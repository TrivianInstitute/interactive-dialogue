# Editorial status

Version 0.1 is a formative, open educational draft. Six modules, activities, sample dialogues, a facilitator plan, a formative rubric, and a research map are available. The curriculum has not been piloted, the rubric is not validated, and no learning or agency outcome is claimed. Sample conversations are explicitly fictional.

Priorities: accessibility review with diverse learners; educator feedback; source review and correction; a consented pilot using independent and delayed transfer checks; revision based on null and adverse results. Proposed study design is in [EVALUATION_PLAN.md](research/EVALUATION_PLAN.md).
