# Formative reasoning rubric

Use for feedback, not ranking people or certifying agency. Score the **artifact in this task**, with agreed accommodations. “Not observed” is different from “absent.”

| Dimension | Emerging | Developing | Demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contribution and responsiveness | Repeats a request or accepts an answer without addressing its content | Responds to a particular point or supplies one original idea | Builds on a point, adds a meaningful constraint or alternative, and revises purposefully |
| Monitoring | Says “I get it” without an explanation or check | Names a gap and tries another representation or example | Explains a gap, changes strategy, and compares confidence with checked work |
| Evidence | Repeats a citation or accepts an unsupported claim | Distinguishes claim from support and inspects a source | States what the source actually supports and qualifies the conclusion |
| Agency | Goal or decision is unclear in the artifact | Sets a goal or makes a choice with one reason | Sets limits, repairs drift when needed, and justifies accept/revise/reject/defer/stop |
| Transfer | Copies the exchange into a new task | Adapts an idea with some independent explanation | Applies the idea in a changed context and explains the adaptation independently |

**Feedback prompts:** What action in the artifact supports this rating? What other explanation might fit? What next attempt would show progress? Record one strength and one next step. Avoid scoring warmth, writing polish, speed, length, identity, preferred belief, or willingness to disclose.

No threshold on this rubric signifies a validated psychological construct, clinical result, or professional credential.
