# Contributing

Contributions that sharpen the educational practice are welcome: corrections, accessible alternatives, source critiques, fictional cases, translations, and reports of where an activity fails. Open an issue or propose a pull request with the file affected, reason, and suggested change. For research claims, provide a primary source and specify its method, population, finding, and limitation. Label a proposed activity as a design choice until it has been evaluated.

Do not submit real learners' transcripts, private information, unpublished manuscripts, or copyrighted source texts without permission. Use invented or fully consented and appropriately redacted examples. Credit outside work accurately and do not imply a linked study endorses this curriculum.

Educational inquiries: learn@trivianinstitute.org. Contributions to a public repository are public; the project license applies to contributions accepted into the materials, subject to contributor rights and third-party restrictions.
