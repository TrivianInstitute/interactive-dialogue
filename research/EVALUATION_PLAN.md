# Evaluation plan: from formative curriculum to evidence

**Status:** proposed; no data collected for this repository. The following is a study design for later review, not a report of results.

## Questions

1. Can learners explain and apply a concept on a new task after a dialogue, without the assistance used during practice?
2. Does confidence better match checked accuracy and explanation quality?
3. Do learners articulate goals, boundaries, and reasons for accept/revise/reject/defer decisions?
4. Who benefits or faces barriers across experience, language, accessibility needs, and setting?

## Feasible pilot

- Recruit a consented group from an appropriate learning setting. Document recruitment, prior familiarity, access accommodations, and attrition. For minors, follow institutional consent and safeguarding requirements.
- Predefine comparable tasks and an answer or scoring guide. Record a baseline attempt and confidence before instruction.
- Compare this curriculum with an appropriate alternative: the same content presented as a conventional worksheet or a direct-answer interaction. If feasible, randomly assign; if not, report selection limits. Keep facilitator time and task difficulty comparable.
- Keep practice performance separate from immediate **independent** post-task and delayed transfer task. State precisely what accommodations and resources remain available in each condition.
- Blind scorers to condition where feasible. Have two scorers independently rate a sample; resolve disagreements and report agreement. Predefine outcomes, exclusions, and analysis before viewing results.
- Report accuracy, explanation quality, confidence versus accuracy, justified decision traces, completion, and unwanted outcomes. A high satisfaction score or number of chat turns is not a learning result.
- Use short interviews to examine misunderstandings, accessibility, usefulness, and experiences of pressure, overtrust, or exclusion. Invite refusal without penalty.

## Candidate observable measures

| Outcome | Candidate measure | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent learning | New equivalent task after access ends | Requires matched difficulty and a checked key |
| Transfer | New context with same underlying idea | Scoring can favor one style unless criteria are explicit |
| Calibration | Confidence recorded before checking vs correctness across multiple items | A single item does not establish stable calibration |
| Agency in task | Written goal, boundary, and reason for a consequential choice | Written performance may not generalize to real-world conduct |
| Dialogue quality | Specific responsiveness, contribution, repair, and stopping | Turn counts and politeness are weak proxies |
| Unwanted effects | Overreliance, mistaken source attribution, distress, privacy concerns | Needs careful, voluntary reporting |

The [formative rubric](../assessment/rubric.md) supports teaching feedback; it is not a validated psychological scale. Before making effectiveness or certification claims, establish appropriate comparison, reliability, accessibility, replication, and limitations. Publish null or adverse results and revise the curriculum accordingly.
