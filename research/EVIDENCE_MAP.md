# Evidence map

| Design choice | Source | Inference made here | What would challenge it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Require the learner to contribute and respond to a specific prior turn | [R1](REFERENCES.md) | Constructive and responsive participation may improve understanding in an AI dialogue | Similar independent learning with a direct answer or more superficial participation |
| Ask for an initial attempt and a later independent explanation | [R2](REFERENCES.md), [R3](REFERENCES.md), [R5](REFERENCES.md) | Monitoring and retrieval may reduce the gap between assisted performance and retained skill | No change or worse transfer on comparable tasks |
| Compare confidence with independently checked work | [R2](REFERENCES.md), [R6](REFERENCES.md) | Explicit checks may help learners notice misplaced certainty | No improvement in calibration or explanation quality |
| Invite alternatives, disagreement, and source checks | [R4](REFERENCES.md), [R7](REFERENCES.md) | Learners may rely on systems more appropriately | Greater confusion, performative disagreement, or no difference in verified decisions |
| Write a boundary and repair scope drift | [R4](REFERENCES.md) | Stated goals and authorization may help retain practical agency | Learners still defer consequential decisions or external actions occur without authorized control |
| Generate multiple possibilities, then adapt one independently | [R1](REFERENCES.md), [R3](REFERENCES.md) | Generative exploration paired with transfer may support original work | Better apparent creativity during AI access but poorer independent adaptation |

Each row is a proposal. The observation in the final column would weaken that proposal, even if participants enjoyed the workshop. See [evaluation plan](EVALUATION_PLAN.md).
