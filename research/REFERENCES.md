# Research references and limits

Reviewed 2026-10-01. Links point to publisher, author institution, proceedings, or indexed records. These studies inform the design; they do **not** validate this curriculum or prove that interactive AI dialogue increases agency or metacognition. Research on human peers, traditional study, or automation must be transferred to AI dialogue cautiously.

## Reading the evidence

- A **framework** organizes concepts and generates hypotheses; it is not an outcome trial.
- An **experiment** can inform causal claims about its studied conditions; transfer to this curriculum remains a new question.
- A **survey** describes reported behavior and associations; it does not show that AI caused a change in an individual's ability.
- A **curriculum design choice** links these findings to an activity through reasoning. Test its effects before treating it as an established intervention.

We cite sources for support and limits, including results that challenge effortless AI-assisted learning. External articles remain the rights of their respective publishers and authors.
