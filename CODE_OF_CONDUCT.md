# Community expectations

Engage claims with curiosity and evidence. Respect a person's right to disagree, pass, revise, or leave. Critique ideas without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or pressure to disclose personal information. Honor access needs and local safeguarding procedures. Do not present educational exercises as therapy, diagnosis, or proof of AI sentience.

If a conversation or contribution violates these expectations, contact learn@trivianinstitute.org with the relevant public link and a brief description. Do not post private identifying details in an issue. Maintainers may edit or remove material and restrict participation to keep the educational space workable.
