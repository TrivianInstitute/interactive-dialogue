# Capstone — design a dialogue that leaves the learner stronger

**60–75 minutes.** Select a real educational task without sensitive information, or use this fictional case: a community group wants to teach newcomers how to evaluate an AI-generated local resource list.

1. Define an achievable learning outcome and a decision the learner retains.
2. Record an initial independent attempt and confidence.
3. Conduct or script a 4–8-turn exchange. Include a clarification, your own contribution, an alternative or objection, and a source-check or scoped reason why none is needed.
4. Repair one misunderstanding or changed constraint. A fictional repair may be inserted if none arose naturally; label it as such.
5. End intentionally. Write a justified choice and a short provenance note.
6. Complete a new, comparable task without consulting the exchange, under stated assistance conditions. Record confidence before checking.
7. Compare both tasks with the [rubric](../assessment/rubric.md). Identify what you can now do, what remains uncertain, and one possible unintended effect.

**Deliverable:** the [dialogue notebook](../templates/dialogue-notebook.md), a checked independent task, and a 150–300 word reflection or equivalent audio/diagrammatic account. Redact personal details before sharing. An unresolved question is an acceptable conclusion.
