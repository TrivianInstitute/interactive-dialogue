# A first dialogue — 15 minutes

**Aim:** feel the difference between receiving an answer and contributing to an inquiry.

Task: plan a 20-minute neighborhood learning exchange. Use paper, a peer, or a conversational AI. With a peer, record that this was peer practice.

1. **2 min:** Write an initial idea, one constraint, and one question.
2. **3 min:** Request or imagine a proposal. Circle an assumption.
3. **5 min:** Respond to a specific part of it. Supply your own alternative and ask how the constraint changes the plan.
4. **3 min:** Choose, revise, or reject an option. State why.
5. **2 min:** Close the exchange. Explain the final idea without looking at the conversation.

**Keep:** initial idea, two responsive turns, final choice, and independent explanation. Notice whether your answer became clearer; avoid inferring learning solely from the number of turns.
