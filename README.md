# Interactive Dialogue

**Learning to think together while remaining the author of your judgment.**

Trivian Institute Educational Commons · v0.1 · Formative educational curriculum

Interactive dialogue is an exchange in which a person asks, interprets, contributes, checks, and revises across turns. A useful conversation makes room for new possibilities, principled disagreement, and a decision to stop. Its value appears in what the learner can understand and do afterward.

This repository teaches dialogue with machine intelligence through six complete modules, fictional examples, printable activities, and a research map. It is designed for adults and older secondary learners, with educator adaptation. No coding, paid subscription, or live AI account is required.

## Start here

- **15 minutes:** [A first dialogue](activities/first-dialogue.md).
- **90 minutes:** [Workshop plan](facilitation/workshop.md).
- **Six sessions:** Work through the modules below, then the [capstone](activities/capstone.md).
- **Research:** [References and limits](research/REFERENCES.md), [evidence map](research/EVIDENCE_MAP.md), and [evaluation plan](research/EVALUATION_PLAN.md).

| Module | Guiding question | Time | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [01 — What dialogue makes possible](modules/01-dialogue.md) | How does an exchange become shared inquiry? | 45 min | Revise an exchange through clarification and contribution |
| [02 — Questions, listening, and meaning](modules/02-questions.md) | What should the next turn do? | 45 min | Choose a question and check mutual interpretation |
| [03 — Metacognition in conversation](modules/03-metacognition.md) | What do I understand, and how can I tell? | 50 min | Compare confidence with independent performance |
| [04 — Disagreement and evidence](modules/04-evidence.md) | What could change this conclusion? | 50 min | Check a claim and revise for a stated reason |
| [05 — Agency, boundaries, and repair](modules/05-agency.md) | Who defines the goal and authorizes the action? | 45 min | Set boundaries, repair a drift, and justify a choice |
| [06 — Creativity and transfer](modules/06-transfer.md) | What can I carry into a new situation? | 50 min | Create alternatives and complete a new task independently |

Use the [dialogue notebook](templates/dialogue-notebook.md), [question cards](templates/question-cards.md), [worked examples](examples/worked-dialogues.md), and [formative rubric](assessment/rubric.md) throughout. See [Foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md) for terms and the proposed practice cycle.

## What the research establishes

Learning research and human–automation research inform individual design choices. Their relevance to this curriculum is explained beside each reference. **This specific curriculum has not been evaluated.** Citations do not establish its scientific validity, and its activities and rubric are not validated measures. We invite testing of independent learning, calibration, and agency rather than claims based on conversational fluency or satisfaction alone.
