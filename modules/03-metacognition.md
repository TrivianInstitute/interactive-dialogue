# 03 — Metacognition in conversation

**50 minutes · Outcome:** detect a gap between feeling familiar and being able to explain.

## Learn

Use a short cycle of planning, monitoring, and reflection, informed by self-regulated learning [R2](../research/REFERENCES.md). Before consulting AI, make an attempt. During the exchange, explain an idea in your own words. Afterward, try a fresh problem with agreed assistance limits.

Self-explanation and retrieval practice inform these activities [R3](../research/REFERENCES.md). These findings do not establish the effectiveness of our conversational adaptation. In one high-school mathematics experiment, AI-assisted practice performance and subsequent independent performance differed [R5](../research/REFERENCES.md); this motivates checking both here.

## Activity: familiarity versus understanding

Materials: notebook; the workshop's checked arithmetic task or another facilitator-checked concept and answer key.

1. **5 min:** Attempt a task independently. Record confidence from 0–100 and why.
2. **10 min:** Ask for a hint or explanation, or study a printed example. Provide your own explanation before asking for feedback.
3. **10 min:** Identify the exact step you cannot explain. Choose a new strategy: diagram, concrete example, simpler case, or human clarification.
4. **8 min:** Close the exchange. Solve a new equivalent task and record confidence before checking the key.
5. **7 min:** Compare accuracy with confidence. State one justified update.
6. **10 min:** Discuss and plan a short retrieval attempt on a later day.

## Exit check

Submit a before/after explanation, checked independent answer, and strategy revision. A confidence increase with an incorrect answer is a reason to investigate, not a learning success.

**Facilitator:** Use checked content within your expertise. Distinguish misunderstanding from language barriers or missing prerequisites. Learners may retain agreed accommodations. One item cannot establish calibration as a stable personal trait.
