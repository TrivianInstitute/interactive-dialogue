# 02 — Questions, listening, and meaning

**45 minutes · Outcome:** choose a useful next turn and repair ambiguity.

## Learn

Useful questions do different jobs: clarify meaning, expose assumptions, explore alternatives, seek evidence, or test application. Start with the gap you need to resolve. Asking for every question type on every turn adds unnecessary work.

Listening in a text exchange means attending to what was actually said. Paraphrase a consequential point and ask whether your interpretation fits. An AI paraphrase is also a proposal to inspect: it may quietly change your goal.

## Activity: the ambiguous brief

Brief: “Help us create an accessible event for new learners.” Fictional reply: “An online evening webinar with an expert speaker is the most accessible option.”

1. **5 min:** List possible meanings of “accessible” and “new learners.”
2. **10 min:** Select three questions from the [question cards](../templates/question-cards.md). Explain the information each could reveal.
3. **10 min:** In pairs, propose answers under different constraints: no stable internet; multilingual participants; limited evening availability. Live AI may propose possibilities if desired.
4. **10 min:** Paraphrase one recommendation, correct an assumption, and request a revision that retains your stated priority.
5. **10 min:** Compare outcomes and complete the exit check.

## Exit check

Submit a question with its purpose, a paraphrase, and a repair turn. Identify one ambiguity that is acceptable to leave unresolved for now.

**Facilitator:** Accept concise or diagrammatic questions. Avoid rewarding elaborate prompt formulas. If the exchange begins to demand personal information, switch to fictional constraints. The purpose is shared interpretation, not disclosure.
